GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

My journey into technology started with Electronics and Telecommunication engineering, where I developed an interest in understanding how technology can be used to solve real-world problems.

During my engineering journey, I worked with embedded systems, IoT and real-time monitoring projects. These experiences taught me how different components work together, how data is generated, and how important reliable data is when building technology-based solutions.

As I explored different areas of technology, I became increasingly interested in the data behind these systems. I started learning Python, SQL, data analysis and visualisation and gradually moved towards machine learning and data engineering.

What interests me most about data is the complete journey of information — from collecting and cleaning raw data, to analysing it, building models, creating dashboards and eventually using it to support better decisions.

My current learning journey focuses on developing practical skills in Data Science and Data Engineering. I am working with technologies such as Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Power BI and Git, while also exploring cloud data platforms, ETL pipelines, Kafka and modern data engineering tools.

I believe that learning technology is most valuable when it is combined with practical work. That is why I am building projects that allow me to apply what I learn instead of only studying concepts theoretically.

My goal is to grow into a data-focused technology role where I can work with real datasets, build reliable data solutions, communicate insights clearly and continue learning from real-world problems.

This portfolio represents that journey. I will continue adding projects, experiments and experiences here as I develop my skills and complete new work.
